# Add the Mile Club button to Chrome (computer)

1. Find the file **mile-club-chrome-extension.zip** and unzip it (double-click it). You now have a folder called **mile-club-chrome-extension**.
2. Open Chrome and type **chrome://extensions** in the address bar, then press Enter.
3. Turn on **Developer mode** (the switch in the top right corner).
4. Click **Load unpacked** (top left) and choose the **mile-club-chrome-extension** folder.
5. Click the puzzle piece icon at the top right of Chrome, then click the pin next to **Mile Club**.

The "mc" button now sits in your toolbar. Click it to open the Mile Club website.

Right now it opens the website preview. When the website is online at your domain, Claude will update the button to open the real address.

Note: extensions only work in Chrome on a computer. On your phone, open the website and use "Add to Home Screen" or "Bookmark" instead.
