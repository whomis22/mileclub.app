About Mile Club

We started Mile Club because running is better with people. Finding a group that runs at your pace, at a time that suits you, shouldn't be hard.

So we built an app that does the finding for you. You turn up, meet people and run.

Coach? Host your own sessions.

Run a track session, a beginners' club or a long-run group, and get paid for it. Mile Club brings the runners to you.
