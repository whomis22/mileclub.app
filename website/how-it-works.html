How it works

From scrolling to showing up.

Mile Club turns “we should do something sometime” into real plans with real people. Here’s how it works, step by step.

  1. Make your profile

    Tell us what you're into, what you do for work, your level and when you're usually free. Add a photo and a few words about you. It takes about two minutes.

  2. Explore what's on

    Your feed shows plans happening near you soon: a padel game tonight, a sunrise run, a pilates class, a spa afternoon. Explore shows what people are posting.

  3. Swipe to join

    Swipe right to join a plan, left to skip. Some plans let you in straight away. Others ask the host first, so groups stay a good fit.

  4. Chat with your group

    Once you're in, you can chat with the group to say hi, share where to meet and sort out the details.

  5. Show up and connect

    Meet your group, try something new and enjoy it. Afterwards, share the moment on your feed and keep in touch with the people you clicked with.

What can you do?

Running is just the start. Mile Club is for anything that's better with other people.

Runs

Easy social jogs, sunrise 5Ks and long weekend runs at every pace.

Padel

Doubles games for beginners and regulars. We find you three more players.

Workouts

Gym sessions, bootcamps and strength classes with people at your level.

Yoga & pilates

Slow down together, from morning flows to reformer classes.

Spa & wellness

Sauna, spa days and cold plunges. Recovery is more fun in company.

Something new

Climbing, dance, boxing, hikes. There is always something you have not tried yet.

Meet people in your field.

Add what you do for work and Mile Club can match you with people in the same industry. Talk shop on a walk, swap ideas between padel games, or just meet people who understand your week.

It's networking without the awkward drinks.

Safe by design.

Every profile can add an emergency contact. You can report or block anyone in one tap, and hosts can choose who joins their plans. Our community guidelines keep Mile Club friendly for everyone.

Questions

Do I need to be fit or sporty?

Not at all. Plans show their level, from total beginner to experienced, so you can pick what feels right. Plenty of plans aren't sporty at all.

Does it cost anything?

Many plans are free to join. Coach-led sessions have a price, which you always see before you book.

Who can join?

Anyone aged 16 or older.

Which cities is Mile Club in?

We're launching one city at a time. If we're not in your city yet, join the waitlist and we'll email you when we arrive.

Can I host my own plans?

Yes. Anyone can create a plan and invite people to join. Coaches can also host paid sessions.
