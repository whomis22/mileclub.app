Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and the choices you have. In plain words.

Last updated 22 November 2026

Mile Club helps you meet people and do activities together. To do that we need some information about you. This page explains, in plain words, what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Mile Club is run by [company name], [registered address], United Kingdom ("we", "us").

What we collect

Account details: your name, @username, email or phone number, and password (stored encrypted). If you use Sign in with Apple, we get the name and email Apple shares with us, which can be a private relay email.

Profile: your photo, bio, age range, home city and neighbourhoods, the activities you like, your level and when you are free.

What you share: posts, comments, likes, photos, chat messages, plans you host or join, and ratings.

Location: only when you choose to, for example to find your city or to share where you are in a chat. We do not track you in the background.

Safety: reports and blocks you make, and no-shows on plans.

Payments: if you buy Premium, Apple or Google handle the payment. We only get a receipt that says you are on Premium. Coach session payments are handled by our payment provider; we never see your full card number.

Device and usage: app version, phone type, crash reports and which screens are used, so we can fix bugs and improve the app.

Camera, microphone, photos and calendar

Camera and microphone are only used when you take a photo, record a post or make a call. Photos are only read when you pick one. Calendar is only used when you tap "Add to calendar". You can turn any of these off at any time in your phone’s Settings.

Why we use it

To run the app: create your account, show your profile, match you with plans and people nearby, and let you chat.

To keep people safe: check reports, enforce the 3 no-shows rule and remove people who break the rules.

To handle Premium and coach payments.

To send you notifications you can turn off in Settings.

To improve Mile Club and fix problems.

Our legal reasons (UK GDPR): to provide the service you signed up for (contract), our legitimate interest in keeping the app safe and working, your consent (for things like location and marketing), and legal duties.

Who can see your information

Other members see your profile, posts and the plans you join. Your phone number and email are never shown to other members.

Service providers who help us run Mile Club, such as hosting and database (Supabase, EU region), app updates (Expo), and payments (Apple, Google and our card payment provider). They can only use your data to provide their service to us.

The police or other authorities, only when the law requires it or to protect someone’s safety.

We never sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile is hidden from everyone straight away. Your data is kept for 1 year in case you change your mind (just log back in to restore it), then deleted for good. You can email us to have it deleted sooner. Some records, like payment receipts, are kept longer when the law says we must.

Your rights

You can see, correct, download or delete your information. You can delete your account any time in Settings → Delete account. You can object to how we use your data or ask us to limit it.

Email hello@mileclub.co and we will reply within one month. If you are not happy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Children

Mile Club is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger.

Keeping it safe and international transfers

We use encryption and access controls to protect your data. Our main servers are in the EU. If data goes outside the UK or EU, we use approved safeguards such as standard contract clauses.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will tell you in the app. Questions? Email hello@mileclub.co.
