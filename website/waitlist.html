What happens next

  1. You join

    Add your name, age, email and city. It takes 20 seconds.

  2. We count

    The more people from a city join, the sooner we launch there.

  3. You get the email

    When Mile Club opens in your city, you're the first to know.

We only use your details to tell you when Mile Club launches near you. You must be 16 or older.

Questions

No. We only email you about Mile Club launching near you.

Can I try the app before it launches in my city?

Yes. You can try the early test version today. See "Try Mile Club today" on the home page.

Can I join for more than one city?

Yes. Just sign up again with the other city.
